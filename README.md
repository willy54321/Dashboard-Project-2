# Dashboard-Project-2
Sales Force Effectiveness Dashboard: Real-Time Revenue Monitoring &amp; Top Sellers  Ranking Analysis Using Excel 

### 1. Executive Summary & Business Context 
This project focused on developing a dynamic sales monitoring solution to help management evaluate sales team effectiveness and achieve annual targets. This dashboard provides full transparency into individual sales managers' contributions and monthly market trends. The primary purpose of this analytical tool is to identify "Top Performers," monitor revenue health in real-time, and ensure product volume sold remains above operational targets. 

### 2. Deep Dive Analysis & Key Findings 

#### A. Main Dashboard (Main Overview & Top Sellers) 
Main Dashboard  
Based on data processed through a dynamic filtering system, the following is an in-depth analysis of individual contributions to the company's total revenue:   
- Top Performer Identification (Daniel Emerson):  
Through ranking visualization, Daniel Emerson was identified as the primary contributor with total sales value of Rp6,514,300. Daniel holds an internal market share of 32% of the total sales generated by the manager team. This indicates a significant performance dependency on one key individual.

- Performance Gap Analysis:  
There is a significant gap between the top and bottom ranks. Emily Rhodes is in second place with Rp6,383,900, indicating intense competition at the top level. However, there is a sharp decline in volume for lower ranked managers like Henry Kim (Rp2,224,500) and Fiona Black (Rp3,021,500). This indicates the need for a training program or customer account redistribution to balance team performance.

- Weekly Sales Efficiency:  
Daily analysis shows that individual maximum sales (Max Weekly Sales) are often reached on Thursdays, with a volume of 1,786 units. This data is crucial for determining coaching schedules or allocating marketing resources on high-intensity days.

- Collective Target Achievement:  
While individual performance varied, collectively, unit sales volume reached 8,695 units, exceeding the operational target of 7,500 units, representing a growth of +16%. This success was driven by the aggressive performance of the Top 2 managers, who offset the volume shortfalls of the other managers.

#### B. Weekly Sales (Daily Trend & Weekly Distribution) 
Dashboard Weekly Sales
This visualization analyzes sales activity over a seven-day cycle to understand short-term 
market behavior and daily operational efficiency. 

- Peak Day Analysis:  
Weekly sales reached their highest point (maximum) on Thursday with a volume of 1,786 units. This significant spike in the middle of the week indicates a stock-building pattern or more active consumer shopping behavior towards the weekend. 

- Selected Day Insight:  
On Wednesday, a transaction value of Rp924,000 was recorded. Although not the day with the highest volume, Wednesday demonstrated a stable revenue contribution that balanced performance before the peak on Thursday.

- Business Insight:  
The uneven distribution of sales between weekdays (high on Thursday) and the beginning of the week indicates the need for more planned operational resource allocation and promotional campaigns on low-sales days to balance the team's workload.
  
#### C. Revenue (Achievement vs. Revenue Target) 
Dashboard Revenue  
Revenue analysis provides vital insight into a company's ability to convert sales volume into a healthy monetary value. 

- Cumulative Revenue Performance:  
Total annual revenue collected reached a significant figure of Rp96,055,000.00. This success was driven by revenue growth of +113%, which far exceeded the previous period's performance standards.

- Monthly Target Gap Analysis:  
Despite positive annual growth, revenue was recorded at Rp6,383,900.00 in February. The dashboard shows a deviation, with monthly operating income falling -33% below the ideal target (Revenue Target of Rp3,000,000.00 per month). 

- Business Insight:  
Aggressive growth of 113% demonstrates the effectiveness of pricing strategies or product mix changes. However, the inability to consistently achieve monthly targets indicates the need to reevaluate overly optimistic target settings or the presence of external factors hindering maximum conversion. 

#### D. Quantity Sold (Unit Sales Volume)
Dashboard Quantity Sold   
This graph charts the movement of product units to gauge the extent to which the market is absorbing available inventory. 

- Annual Sales Volume:  
The company recorded an impressive total unit sales volume of 99,994 units throughout the year. In February, unit sales reached 8,695 units, exceeding the monthly sales target of 7,500 units.

- Efficiency Against Target:  
Overall, market absorption grew by +16% above the established unit target. This success in exceeding the physical target indicates strong market demand for the product.

- Business Insight:  
An interesting correlation exists where unit volume grew above target (+16%), but monthly revenue sometimes fell below target (-33%). This technical finding indicates that the high volume may be driven by low-margin products or an aggressive discounting strategy, meaning that high volume does not necessarily translate into revenue that aligns with financial targets. 

### 3. Strategic Recommendations & Business Impact 
Based on an in-depth analysis of sales performance, unit volume, and daily distribution, the following strategic recommendations are available to improve the company's efficiency and profitability:  

#### A. Margin Optimization and Pricing Structure 
- Recommendation:  
Given that unit sales volume exceeded the target (+16%) but monthly revenue fell below target (-33%), the company needs to reevaluate its discount strategy and product mix. The focus should be shifted to promoting higher-margin products or bundling products to increase average transaction value. 

- Business Impact:  
This adjustment will ensure that aggressive unit volume growth is directly proportional to revenue growth, thereby consistently achieving the monthly financial target of IDR 3,000,000.00.

#### B. Operational Synchronization and Stock Management 
- Recommendation:  
Utilize peak weekly sales data on Thursday (1,786 units) to optimize supply chain management. The logistics team should ensure maximum stock availability in the warehouse on Wednesday evening to prevent potential lost sales due to stockouts.

- Business Impact:  
Accurate stock fulfillment on peak days will improve customer satisfaction and maximize daily market absorption, which is currently the primary driver of annual volume of 99,994 units.

#### C. Capacity Building and Sales Team Balancing 
- Recommendation:  
Reduce performance dependency on key individuals (Top Sellers) by implementing a mentoring program or knowledge sharing from Daniel Emerson (32% contribution) with other team members. Use the dashboard filter system to identify specific areas where underperforming managers need guidance.

- Business Impact:  
Balancing team performance will create long-term revenue stability. If all managers can achieve the same efficiency standards, the company can project significantly higher annual revenue growth than the current Rp96,055,000.00.

#### D. Financial Target Review (Revenue Calibration) 
- Recommendation:  
Recalibrate monthly revenue targets based on historical data of +113% annual growth. Targets should be made more dynamic by considering seasonal factors as seen in the monthly trend chart. 
- Business Impact:  
More realistic and measurable targets will increase sales team motivation and provide stakeholders with a more accurate picture of cash flow projections for future investment planning.

### 4. Conclusion 
This Sales Dashboard with Top Seller Filter provides much-needed visibility for management to understand that high sales volume (99,994 units) does not necessarily guarantee the achievement of monthly revenue targets. Overall, the business demonstrated strong financial health with annual growth of +113%. However, by implementing the above recommendations particularly synchronizing Thursday operations and improving the pricing structure the company was able to close the efficiency gap by 33% in monthly revenue. This project demonstrates that data-driven decision-making is key to converting sales volume into sustainable profitability.
